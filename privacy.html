Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short

Garantim keeps your receipts and products on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, so we never receive your data.

Who we are

Garantim is made by an independent developer: Mert Kandemir, Erler Caddesi 83/1 Yaman Apt., 35310 İzmir, Türkiye. Contact: mertkandemir01@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Receipt photos, the text read from them, your products, warranty dates, trips, tax free notes and settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device.

Receipts are read on the device with Apple’s Vision framework. Images and text are not sent anywhere.

Deleting a product deletes its receipt photo. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera and your photo library only so you can scan or pick a receipt. It never reads other photos.

Notifications

Warranty, return and trip reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

Garantim Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account. We cannot access it.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is under your control in the app. For any privacy question under the GDPR, KVKK or other law, write to mertkandemir01@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
